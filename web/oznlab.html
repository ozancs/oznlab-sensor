    What you are looking at

    The coil sees the hotend move by fractions of a micrometre. Push the cold nozzle up with a finger and the line jumps; during a tap it rises as the nozzle meets the bed; while extruding it follows the melt pressure. At rest it should be flat.

    Measure pressure advance

    The extruder pulses slow and fast in the air while K goes up. The answer is the last K whose pressure does not dip below its new level after a speed drop. About 2 to 4 minutes and 15 to 25 cm of filament.

    Pressure after each speed drop

    One line per K, 0 is the new level. Low K stays above it, too much K dips below the red line.

    Z offset

    A tap measures where the nozzle meets the bed and makes Z 0 there, plus the squish. The G-code offset is not touched: what you see there is your babystep only. The sensor gets stiffer as the hotend heats, so the tap also records how many Hz one micrometre of push gives.

    Sensitivity by temperature

    Hz per micrometre of nozzle push, from the taps. Homing and the crash watch use these.

    Tap repeatability

    Ten taps on the same spot, nothing is changed. The narrower the spread, the more the tap can be trusted. A clean nozzle tip matters most.

    Tests

    Each test shows its result under its button, and in the console. The filament tests extrude in the air: park the nozzle over the purge area first.

    Push test

    Push the nozzle up with a finger for 30 s. Every push prints a line with its size.

    Cold nozzle only.
    Max flow

    Extrudes faster and faster and finds where the hotend stops keeping up. Use the answer as the slicer's max volumetric speed.

    Retraction

    Finds the retraction length that drops the melt pressure, the one that stops oozing without wasting time.

    Temperature scan

    Pressure against nozzle temperature: where this filament flows freely without cooking.

    Crash test

    Knock the toolhead for 60 s and see if each hit is noticed.

    Homing dry run

    Runs the nozzle homing descent on a homed printer without changing anything.

    Health check

    Wiring, the chip, the coil, the config and the stored values, each with what to do when it is off.
